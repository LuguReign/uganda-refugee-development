The research question

How does the relationship between refugee presence and school access change over time, and does that change differ with initial primary-school access?

Main interpretation: the primary-school exposure relationship strengthens by 2020. The standardized extension does not establish a difference between baseline-access groups at the 5% level. A raw-unit check changes that inference, so heterogeneity remains specification-sensitive.

Assumptions and limitations

Settlement location is not random. Causal interpretation depends on conditional parallel trends, measurement comparability, and limited confounding or spillovers. Refugee arrivals occur together with humanitarian aid and policy responses. Parish clustering does not address all geographic dependence.

Missing controls exclude 1,055 rows. Health-tier measures stop in 2016 and road measures start in 2011. No imputation fills those structural gaps. The archive's binary binning excludes another seven observations.

Positive relative school-access relationships coexist with a decline in unweighted average raw access. Neither pattern alone establishes a causal national-level effect. Exploratory comparisons are not adjusted for multiple testing.
